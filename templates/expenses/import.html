CSV Format Rules

Your CSV must have these 4 columns (header row required):

date — YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY
category — e.g. Food & Dining
description — what was the expense
amount — plain number, e.g. 1500.00
Column order doesn't matter. Extra columns are ignored. Unknown categories are created automatically.
Sample Format
date category description amount
2025-03-01 Food & Dining Naivas groceries 3850
2025-03-05 Transport Uber to airport 1800
2025-03-10 Housing & Utilities KPLC bill 2300
